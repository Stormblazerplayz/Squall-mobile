# Unread counts and Mark as unread

- The row of teammates above a chat now shows the red count of new messages from the computer, like the chat list on iPad does. A draft pencil shows when there's nothing new.
- Press and hold a teammate (in the row, or in the chat list on iPad) and pick **Mark as unread** to keep a chat as something to come back to. It shows a red dot until you open that chat. **Mark as read** takes it off.
- Marks are kept on this phone. Removing a teammate or Forget everything drops them.

Code: `Squall/Models/UnreadMarks.swift`, `Squall/Views/Chat/UnreadBadge.swift`.
