# Bubble shape

Settings → Chat → **Bubble shape** changes how messages look:

- **Round**: the usual look.
- **Tails**: your bubbles get a small corner at the bottom right, and a teammate's reply gets one at the top left pointing
  at their picture, like a texting app. A reply that follows another from the same teammate has no tail.
- **Square**: crisp, nearly square corners.

A small preview under the switch shows the pick right away. The press-and-hold preview follows the same shape.

Code: `Squall/Models/BubbleStyle.swift`, used in `MessageView` and Settings.
