# Problems in this chat

Tap ••• at the top of a chat and pick **Browse this chat** > **Problems** (or press ⌘⇧P) to see every problem the teammate hit in that chat, newest first: the first line of the error, when it happened, and ×3 when it happened several times in a row.

- Tap a problem to copy the whole error, ready to paste into a message asking for a fix.
- Press and hold for Go to message, which jumps to it in the chat.
- A chat with no problems says so with a green check.
- Press and hold and pick **Ask Rex to fix it** to put a short ask and the error, as a quote, in the chat's message box. Nothing is sent until you tap Send, so you can add to it first.
