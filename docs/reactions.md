# Message reactions

Press and hold your message or a teammate's reply and pick **React**, then one of 👍 ❤️ 😂 🎉 🔥 👀.
The emoji hangs off the bottom corner of the bubble. Tap it to pick another one or take it off.

- One reaction per message. Picking the same one again takes it off.
- Reactions are kept on this phone only, like a sticky note for yourself. Teammates don't see them.
- They go when a teammate leaves the team, and with Forget everything.

Code: `Squall/Models/MessageReactions.swift`, `Squall/Views/Chat/ReactionViews.swift`.
