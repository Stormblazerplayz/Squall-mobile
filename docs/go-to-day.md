# Go to a day

Tap ••• at the top of a chat and pick **Go to a day**. A list shows every day the chat has messages on, newest first:

- the date in a small calendar tile, "Today", "Yesterday" or "Monday, Nov 23" (with the year when it's not this year)
- how many messages that day had
- the day's first words

Tap a day and the chat scrolls to its first message and lights it up with a ring for a moment.

Days come from the message times the computer sends. A computer that doesn't send times shows "No dated messages yet".

Code: `Squall/Models/ChatDayJump.swift`, `Squall/Views/Chat/ChatDaySheet.swift`.
