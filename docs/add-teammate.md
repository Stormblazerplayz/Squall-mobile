# Add teammate

Settings > Your team > **Add teammate** brings a new teammate to life from the phone, the same as on the computer.

- **Start from:** Coder, Researcher, Writer and the rest fill in the role, what they do and a color.
- **Name:** type one or roll the dice for a free one. 2 to 20 letters and digits, starting with a letter.
- **Role, color and what they do:** all optional, with a live preview card at the top.
- **Bring to life:** the computer adds them, they say hello in their chat, and the button turns into "Open their chat".

The team holds 12 at most. The row only shows when Squall on the computer can add teammates
(squall-computer docs/phone-add-teammate.md).

Code: `TeamAdd` in Squall/Models/TeamAdd.swift and the views in Squall/Views/Settings/AddTeammateViews.swift.
