# Chat stats

Tap ••• at the top of a chat and pick **Browse this chat** > **Chat stats**. It's worked out on the phone from the chat's own messages:

- how many messages, and since when
- a bar showing who sends more, you or the teammate
- your words and the teammate's words, code blocks, problems, days active and the busiest hour
- a small chart of messages by hour of the day
- the longest reply, with how long it takes to read. Tap **Go to it** to jump there.

Code: `Squall/Models/ChatStats.swift`, `Squall/Views/Chat/ChatStatsSheet.swift`.
