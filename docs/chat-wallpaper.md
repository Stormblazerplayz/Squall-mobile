# Chat wallpaper

Tap ••• at the top of a chat, then **Wallpaper**, and pick Dots, Grid, Waves, Stars or Stripes. The pattern is drawn
softly in the teammate's own color behind the messages and fades a little toward the bottom so replies stay easy to read.

- **Use … in every chat** puts the same wallpaper behind every chat and takes off each chat's own pick.
- Pick **None** to go back to the plain glow.
- Kept on this phone. A teammate who leaves the team takes their pick with them, and Forget everything clears it.

Code: `Squall/Models/ChatWallpapers.swift`, `Squall/Views/Chat/ChatWallpaperView.swift`.
