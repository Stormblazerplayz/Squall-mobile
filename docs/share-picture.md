# Share as picture

Press and hold any of your messages or a teammate's reply and pick **Share as picture**. Squall makes a card with the
teammate's sphere and name, the time, the reply with its formatting, and a small Squall mark, then opens the share
sheet so you can save it to Photos or send it in Messages.

- The picture is made on the phone only when you pick where it goes. Nothing is uploaded.
- Your own messages use your accent color. A teammate's reply uses theirs.
- Very long replies are cut at a word (about 1,200 characters) and end with "…" so they fit one picture.

Code: `Squall/Views/Chat/MessagePicture.swift`, the menu item is in `MessageMenu` (`StarredViews.swift`).
