# Big emoji

A message that is only one, two or three emoji shows big, like in a texting app. Yours has no bubble at all; a teammate's
reply shows the emoji big inside its card. One emoji is the biggest, three are a bit smaller.

Digits, # and * don't count as emoji on their own, and a message with any words stays a normal bubble.

Code: `Squall/Models/JumboEmoji.swift`, used in `MessageView`.
