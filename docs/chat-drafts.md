# Chat drafts

Start typing to a teammate, switch to someone else, and come back: what you typed is still there. It's kept even if you
close Squall.

- The chat strip shows a small pencil on teammates with something typed and not sent.
- On iPad, the chat list shows "Draft: ..." under their name.
- Sending the message, or clearing the box, removes the draft.
- Drafts are kept on this phone only, and go away when a teammate is removed or the phone is unpaired.

Code: `Squall/Models/ChatDrafts.swift` (used in `ChatView`). Tests: `SquallTests/ChatDraftsTests.swift`.
